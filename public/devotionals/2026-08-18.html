Screen Out The Lies

Pastor David Ofurum

Colossians 2:4 KJV And this I say, lest any man should beguile you with enticing words.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Be careful. Be vigilant. Not everyone speaking in My name is speaking for Me. Heretics have multiplied and are speaking pleasant words that are soothing to the ears but damning to the soul. Be diligent to search the Scriptures, sift through what you hear and discard any falsehood in it.

TO DO: PRAY: Dear Holy Spirit, help me to keep guard over my spirit against enticing words spoken in Your name. Help me build a sound doctrinal foundation on Your word upon which truth alone will stand, heresies will be exposed, and deception will fall to pieces in Jesus’ name. Amen.
