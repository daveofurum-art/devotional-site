Tame Your Tongue

Pastor David Ofurum

James 3:10 KJV Out of the same mouth proceedeth blessing and cursing. My brethren, these things ought not so to be.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Never let your tongue get out of control, or your life will get out of control. What you say either justifies or condemns you. It is also absurd to let profanities flow from the same mouth you use in singing praise and worship to Me. Like seriously? Never normalize that. Tame your tongue.

TO DO: CONFESS: My mouth belongs to Jesus because my body is the temple of the Holy Spirit. Therefore, I will use my tongue to honour God alone and to speak His will into existence in my life. As God is my help, no F-word, S-word, or any other swear word will come from my mouth. I take charge of my mouth.
