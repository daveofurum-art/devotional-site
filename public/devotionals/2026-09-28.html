Gifted for My Glory

Pastor David Ofurum

1 Kings 5:7 KJV And it came to pass, when Hiram heard the words of Solomon, that he rejoiced greatly, and said, Blessed be the LORD this day, which hath given unto David a wise son over this great people.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Every gift, talent, or ability I give you is meant to positively impact your world, inspire the ungodly to seek Me, and glorify My name. They are not excuses to become like the world but tools to draw the world to Me, and once you blur the line between those two, you fail in your purpose and fail Me.

TO DO: DECLARE: I will use everything God has given me to glorify His name in very clear terms. The world will see His light in me. In using my abilities, I will not conform to the world. My mission is to transform the world with my gifts, and as God is my help, that will always be my focus and result. Amen.
