It's Only A Phase

Pastor David Ofurum

Romans 8:18 KJV For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed in us.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: You may be going through sufferings and pains right now, but that’s not how your story will end. This is only a phase, and it shall pass. Moreover, this process will produce much praise and beauty beyond your imagination. Mark My words and don’t lose hope. You’re coming out better.

TO DO: CONFESS: I know, and I believe that this is not the end. Weeping may endure for a night, but my joy is coming in the morning. When I am tried, I shall come forth like pure gold. This shall pass. Change is coming. Glory is rising. I shall tell the story of how I overcame. It shall end in praise. Hallelujah!
