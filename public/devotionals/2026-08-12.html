It Only Takes Faith

Pastor David Ofurum

John 3:14 KJV And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Whoever looked up to the brazen serpent Moses made in the wilderness was saved from snakebite death. How can seeing an object be an antidote? It doesn’t make sense; it only took faith. Similarly, only faith in Jesus saves from sin and ushers you into eternal life. It doesn’t have to make sense to you; it only takes faith.

TO DO: DECLARE: Whether it’s logical or not that salvation only comes from one person, I choose to put my faith in that one person, Whom You, O God, have appointed as the sole Source of salvation. My faith will forever be in Jesus Christ. Anyone who wishes to debate logic may proceed; I choose to deploy faith.
