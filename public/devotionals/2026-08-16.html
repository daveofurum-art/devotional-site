Warning: Leave Now

Pastor David Ofurum

Micah 2:10 KJV Arise ye, and depart; for this is not your rest: because it is polluted, it shall destroy you, even with a sore destruction.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: I have told you before; now I warn you: Do not settle down in that place, else it will be your ruin. Do not be deceived by how rosy it seems now; it’s only a trap. Having seen the end from the beginning, I warn you to leave before it’s too late. Remember Lot, how he chose Sodom, and it destroyed him. Do not make his mistake.

TO DO: DECLARE: Lord, I humble myself before You. I will heed Your warning and do as You say. I will not be seduced by the deceitful pleasures of iniquity to remain in a place where I ought not to be. Thank You for the courage to end this foolishness. I’m packing my bags and leaving in Jesus’ name. Amen.
