A Reason To Praise<

Pastor David Ofurum

Psalm 150:6 KJV Let every thing that hath breath Praise the LORD. Praise ye the LORD.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: You’re breathing. That’s why you can read or hear these words. So, you have a very good reason to praise Me. No matter your situation, you have hope for a better tomorrow; for a living dog is better than a dead lion. Don’t let Satan and self-pity steal your joy. Get up and praise Me; change is coming.

TO DO: DECLARE: I’ll praise in the valley and on the mountain, when I’m sure and when I’m uncertain, when outnumbered and surrounded, when I feel it and when I don’t, because I know, Lord, that You’re still in control. As long as I’m breathing, I’ve got a reason to praise the Lord. Hallelujah!
