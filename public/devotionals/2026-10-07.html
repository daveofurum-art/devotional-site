Maturity First

Pastor David Ofurum

1 Timothy 3:6 KJV Not a novice, lest being lifted up with pride he fall into the condemnation of the devil.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Ministry is not for the spiritually immature but for persons who have depth in Me. Pride is one major indicator of immaturity. If your anointing or accomplishments puff up your heart, you’re not ripe for ministry yet; otherwise, you will fall in Lucifer style. Anointing without humility is immaturity and will lead to destruction.

TO DO: PRAY: Search my heart, O Lord. Find and uproot any trace of pride in me. Purge me of self-conceit and self-aggrandizement. Keep me remembering that I am nothing and can achieve nothing without You. Teach me to always glorify You, not myself, for Your ability at work in me, in Jesus’ name. Amen.
