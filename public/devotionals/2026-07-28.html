My Best Love

Pastor David Ofurum

Romans 8:32 KJV He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things?

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Think deeply about this and let it give you a fresh perspective of My love for you. What could be more precious to Me than My Son whom I sacrificed for you? What then can I withhold from you? There’s nothing you can do to make Me love you more or give you more; I already gave you everything.

TO DO: DECLARE: Father, I stand amazed at the depth of Your love for me. I recognize that the benefits I enjoy from You are not the results of my works, but the products of Your unreserved love so lavishly poured on me. You’ll never love me more than You do right now because You’ve always loved me Your best and always will. Thank You, Lord.
