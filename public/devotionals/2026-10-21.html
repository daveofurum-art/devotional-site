Position for Greatness

Pastor David Ofurum

Romans 4:12 KJV And the father of circumcision to them who are not of the circumcision only, but who also walk in the steps of that faith of our father Abraham, which he had being yet uncircumcised.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Abraham walked in faith, and the faith he practiced alone has become a global practice today. As you walk in obedience to My precepts, your private obedience today could become a global influence tomorrow. You position yourself for greatness by living in faith and obedience. So, don’t relent.

TO DO: DECLARE: Father, I receive the grace to stay on the path of obedience in the seemingly little things that You have instructed me to practice. My goal is not global prominence but pleasing You. Do with my obedience whatever You will on the global stage, both now and in the future, in Jesus’ name. Amen.
