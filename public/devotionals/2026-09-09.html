Rise Above Grief

Pastor David Ofurum

Joshua 1:2 KJV Moses my servant is dead; now therefore arise, go over this Jordan, thou, and all this people, unto the land which I do give to them, even to the children of Israel.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: A loss can be very painful and cause great difficulty, but don’t let it hold you back. You must come to terms with it, pick yourself up, and forge ahead. You have so much destiny ahead that you can’t afford to stay down for too long. Grieve, yes, but move on. I am still with you, and I’m more than what you lost.

TO DO: PRAY: Holy Spirit, my Comforter, You Who understand my loss and pain more than I do, help me to rise above my grief and move on to the great promise ahead of me. With You, I stand strong and brave, knowing that my future will never look like what I’ve been through, in Jesus’ name. Amen.
