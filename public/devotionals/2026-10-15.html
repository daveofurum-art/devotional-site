Don't Be a Lone Ranger

Pastor David Ofurum

Ecclesiastes 4:12 KJV And if one prevail against him, two shall withstand him; and a threefold cord is not quickly broken.

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Isolation is one tactic your adversary, the devil, uses to target his prey. If he succeeds in separating you from the support system you do need, then he can launch a brutal assault to devour you. Don’t be a lone ranger. The idea that you need no one is deception. The more connected you are to like minds, the better.

TO DO: PRAY: Heavenly Father, help me to value the network of family, friends, associates, Church members, and other support groups You have provided for me. Help me eliminate the foolish arrogance of self-sufficiency. Teach me to accept help and support when I need them in Jesus’ name. Amen.
