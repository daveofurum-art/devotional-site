Good Old Days?

Pastor David Ofurum

John 4:12 KJV Art thou greater than our father Jacob, which gave us the well, and drank thereof himself, and his children, and his cattle?

THUS SAYS THE LORD: With Me, something better is always lying ahead. Don’t be stuck on what you think are “good old days” when your best is yet to come. Just because your ancestors passed down a great tradition to you doesn’t mean it fits in today’s realities. Be open to new and better ways I can show you.

TO DO: PRAY: Precious Holy Spirit, the Master promised that You would guide me into all truth. I am open to Your guidance. If there are things You’d have me do differently from what I have long known and practiced, Lord, teach me more excellent ways that please the Father in Jesus’ name. Amen.
