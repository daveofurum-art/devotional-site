This Is Your Sign

Pastor David Ofurum

Judges 4:6 KJV And she sent and called Barak the son of Abinoam out of Kedesh-naphtali, and said unto him, Hath not the LORD God of Israel commanded, saying, Go and draw toward mount Tabor, and take with thee ten thousand men of the children of Naphtali and of the children of Zebulun?

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Have I not commanded you to take on that challenge headlong? You’ve been asking Me for a sign, a confirmation that you will succeed and the risk you take will not be in vain. Now, you’re reading this. This is your sign. This is your confirmation. Go, and I will be with you.

TO DO: DECLARE: Holy Father, I thank You for confirming Your command to me. I might not have all the details of what lies ahead. But one thing I know: If You’ve sent me, then You’re with me, and Your presence is all I need. I go as commanded, and I thank You in advance for the victory in Jesus’ name. Amen.
