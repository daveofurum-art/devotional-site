Succeed In Ministry

Pastor David Ofurum

Ephesians 4:12 KJV For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ:

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Every minister of mine has their job clearly cut out for them. They all serve one and the same purpose: to equip the saints for effective ministry. So, the yardstick for measuring ministry success is: Am I helping God’s people mature and become more effective in serving Him? Nothing else is as important as this.

TO DO: PRAY: Father, help Your ministers stay focused on their main calling – to groom every saint into a minister. Uproot every distraction that competes with Your purpose for ministry. Help Your ministers access the provisions You have made to support their success in ministry, in Jesus' name. Amen.
