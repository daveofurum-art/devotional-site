Your Time Has Come

Pastor David Ofurum

Genesis 8:1 KJV And God remembered Noah, and every living thing, and all the cattle that was with him in the ark: and God made a wind to pass over the earth, and the waters asswaged;

THUS SAYS THE LORD: Cheer up, son, your time of remembrance has come. Get ready, daughter, your long wait is over. I am putting a new song in your mouth; you, your household, and everyone around you will celebrate together because of your testimony. Before this month is over, you will sing and dance for joy.

TO DO: DECLARE: Thank You, Adonai, for giving me a new season and a new song. Thank You for paying special attention to my matter. Thank You for bringing an end to the storms and delays. Because of You, Lord, my story is changing, and men will remember me for good. Thank You, Abba Father.
